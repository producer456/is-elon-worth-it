What a trillion is taken from

A trillion dollars doesn't appear from nowhere. It's the same size as the things we all live on — schools, food aid, healthcare, retirement. So when that much goes to one person, it's not just a number on a screen. It's that many years of this, taken from everyone else.

So — is he worth it?

You've seen both sides of the same trillion. Now it's a judgment call, and it's yours.

The case for
He built companies that put rockets in orbit and EVs on the road, employs hundreds of thousands, and the package only pays out if he hits enormous targets that make others rich too. Reward the upside, and you get more of it.
The case against
No one person does a trillion dollars of work. That same sum is a year of Medicare, or food aid for a decade, or 83 years of Head Start. Concentrating it in one pair of hands is wealth removed from everyone else's.
We forget that giving that much to one person
is exactly what we take away from the rest of us.
